Voices of Hope Music Promotions was founded with a singular purpose — to elevate the standard of choral and praise music throughout Zambia, creating platforms where voices can be heard, celebrated and developed.

"We set the standard of excellence in musical promotion. We fulfil our promises." — Rev. Daniel Kombe, Chairperson

Our Legacy

The Yvonne Kawimbe
Memorial Music Competition

A prestigious annual competition held in honour of Yvonne Chinakila Mulenga Kawimbe — celebrating excellence, faith and the power of music.

🎤

In Loving Memory of Yvonne Chinakila Mulenga Kawimbe

This annual competition celebrates her legacy and lasting contribution to music ministry in Zambia. Each edition honours her memory by inspiring new generations of musicians to reach for excellence and minister through song.

2025

1st Annual Competition

The inaugural Yvonne Chinakila Mulenga Kawimbe Memorial Music Competition — bringing together choirs from across Zambia for the first time on this prestigious platform.

✓ Successfully Held
2026

2nd Annual Competition

Building on the success of 2025, the 2026 edition raised the bar — held at Ebenezer Cathedral AMEC, Chilenje on 16th May 2026. Expanding reach, growing participation, and cementing this competition as Zambia's premier choral event.
