Voices of Hope Music Promotions was founded with a singular purpose — to elevate the standard of choral and praise music throughout Zambia, creating platforms where voices can be heard, celebrated and developed.

"We set the standard of excellence in musical promotion. We fulfil our promises." — Rev. Daniel Kombe, Chairperson

Our Core Values

Built on principles

  • ✨
    Creativity We believe great music begins with creative freedom and fearless expression.
  • 💡
    Innovation Bringing fresh approaches to how music competitions and workshops are designed and delivered.
  • 🤝
    Transparency Fair, open and honest in everything we do — from judging to partnerships.

What We Do

Elevating every voice

From competitions that inspire excellence to workshops that build skill — we create the platforms where Zambia's musical talent shines.

🏆

Music Competitions

Professionally organised choral and praise team competitions that set the highest standard of musical excellence throughout Zambia.

🎓

Music Workshops

Skill-building workshops led by experienced practitioners — equipping choirs and praise teams with the tools to grow and excel.

📣

Music Promotions

Amplifying the voices of exceptional choirs and ensembles — helping talented groups reach the audiences they deserve.
